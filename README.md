# 🚆 RailTwin: Digital Twin Software to Explain Block Scheduling of Railway Maintenance

## 📌 Project Overview

**RailTwin** is a Digital Twin-based software concept designed to explain and visualize block scheduling in railway maintenance. It provides a virtual representation of railway tracks, trains, and maintenance activities to help users understand how railway maintenance blocks are planned and scheduled.

The system aims to simulate railway operations during maintenance periods, showing how track availability, train movements, and maintenance schedules are coordinated to minimize railway disruptions.

By using digital simulation and visualization, RailTwin helps railway operators understand scheduling decisions, identify possible conflicts, and improve maintenance planning.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Develop a digital representation of railway infrastructure.
- Visualize railway maintenance block scheduling.
- Understand the relationship between train movements and maintenance activities.
- Identify scheduling conflicts between trains and maintenance operations.
- Minimize railway service disruptions.
- Improve railway maintenance planning and resource utilization.

## ⚙️ Key Features

### 1. Digital Twin Visualization
Provides a virtual representation of railway tracks and railway operations.

### 2. Block Scheduling
Displays how railway tracks are allocated for maintenance during specific time periods.

### 3. Train Movement Simulation
Explains how train movements are affected when particular railway sections are blocked for maintenance.

### 4. Maintenance Planning
Helps visualize maintenance activities, available track sections, and scheduled maintenance periods.

### 5. Conflict Identification
Helps identify potential conflicts between train operations and maintenance schedules.

### 6. Interactive Dashboard
Provides a visual interface to understand railway maintenance schedules and operational status.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The proposed system consists of the following components:

1. **Input Data:** Railway track information, train schedules, and maintenance requirements.
2. **Digital Twin Model:** Creates a virtual representation of railway infrastructure.
3. **Scheduling Module:** Represents maintenance block allocation and scheduling.
4. **Simulation Engine:** Simulates railway operations during maintenance periods.
5. **Visualization Module:** Displays railway tracks, train movements, and maintenance blocks.
6. **Dashboard:** Presents scheduling information and simulation results.

## 🔄 Working Process

1. Collect railway track, train timetable, and maintenance information.
2. Create a digital representation of the railway network.
3. Define maintenance requirements and time slots.
4. Allocate railway blocks for maintenance activities.
5. Simulate train movements during maintenance periods.
6. Identify possible scheduling conflicts.
7. Visualize the results through an interactive dashboard.
